About this template

Built for departments who rotate trainees — not this exact one

Acute medicine trainees rotate between placements every few months, and QIPs tend to rotate with them — started, half-finished, and quietly abandoned when a placement ends. A page like this one exists to break that cycle: a shared record of what’s been tried, what worked, and who to ask.

Everything below — the sites, the stats, the QIP list — is placeholder content. Replace it with your own department’s, and this becomes a working home page for your network, not a demo of someone else’s.

Focus areas trainees are working on

8 categories

QI-01

NEWS2 & escalation

Timeliness and documentation of escalation when NEWS2 triggers are reached on the acute take.

QI-02

VTE risk assessment

Completion of VTE risk assessment within 14 hours of admission.

QI-03

AKI care bundle

Bundle compliance and time to senior review for stage 2–3 AKI flagged on admission bloods.

QI-04

Delirium screening

4AT completion on admission for patients aged 65+.

QI-05

Sepsis Six timing

Door-to-antibiotic time and Sepsis Six bundle completion within the golden hour.

QI-06

Discharge summaries

Turnaround time and GP-relevant content of discharge summaries.

QI-07

Medicines reconciliation

Reconciliation within 24 hours of admission, with a pharmacy cross-check on high-risk drugs.
